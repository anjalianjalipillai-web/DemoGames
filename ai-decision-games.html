Things like calculators, light switches and vending machines always follow the exact same fixed rule. AI-powered tools instead look at data — like your viewing history or writing style — and change their decision based on patterns they find in it.

What you learned

AI doesn't "think" or "feel" — it looks for patterns in lots of examples (called training data), and uses those patterns to make a decision. Because it learns from examples, it can sometimes get things wrong, especially if its examples were unfair or incomplete.
